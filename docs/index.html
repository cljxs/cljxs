Clip

A personal tool run by one person for their own channels.

Clip turns long videos that its owner has the rights to use into short vertical clips with captions. Rights here means the owner's own recordings, footage from creators who have given permission, or openly licensed works. Every clip is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is posted. Approved clips are posted to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services.

Clip is not offered to the public. It does not let anyone else sign in, and it does not collect data from the people who watch the clips.

How it uses YouTube

  • Uploads a video the owner has approved to the owner's channel, with the title, description and tags the owner has reviewed.
  • Reads public statistics for trending videos and channels, to decide which moments of permitted footage to clip.

Clip uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
