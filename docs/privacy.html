Privacy policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Clip is a personal tool run by one person (the "owner") to turn videos they have the rights to use into short clips and post them to their own channels. It is not offered to the public, and nobody but the owner signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

Clip uses YouTube API Services. By using Clip you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Clip accesses

  • The owner's YouTube account, with their permission. When the owner signs in, Google gives Clip a token. Clip uses it only to upload videos the owner has approved to the owner's channel, and to read the status of those uploads.
  • Public YouTube data. This means video titles, descriptions and view counts, and channel names and subscriber counts. Clip reads them to see which creators and moments are trending.

Clip does not access anyone else's account, does not read private data, and collects nothing about the people who watch the clips.

Where it is kept

The sign-in token and Clip's records (which videos were processed, which clips were made and posted) are stored on the owner's own private server. The token file can be read only by the owner's account on that server. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising.

Who else sees anything

  • YouTube receives the videos the owner approves, with their titles, descriptions and tags.
  • An AI model provider (OpenRouter) receives the transcript text of a clip, only to draft its title, caption and hashtags. No account data or token is sent.

How long, and how to remove it

The token is kept until the owner removes it or revokes Clip's access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When it is revoked, Clip can no longer use the account, and the owner deletes the stored token. Records of processed videos are kept for the owner's own use and deleted when no longer needed.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: github.com/cljxs.
