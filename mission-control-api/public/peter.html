How to read a card
Each percentage is how often he has cleared that bar in his own games over two seasons, smoothed, with its 90% range underneath - how far it moves when his games are resampled, so a small sample says so. It is what he does when he plays: PLAYED n/m marks the rows where he has missed games. A sportsbook's line, where one is posted, is shown in blue to read beside it; no percentage was computed from it. LAST 5 is his five most recent games of that stat - a glance, not a model. The weather is shown, not used: no percentage moves with it.
